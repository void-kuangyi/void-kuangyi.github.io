Extra Organum
Year: 2026
Interactive installation
latex, 3d print, webcam, touchdesigner, projection, medicine pills

Coils, Pills, Bills, Extra Organum
Vids, Slurs, Force, Extra Organum
Signs, Rules, Laws, Extra Organum

Extra Organum is an interactive installation on everyday external and internal body surveillance and how they cause alienation and mutation of our bodies. From contraceptive coils and pills, to judgement and assault, to rules and legislation, they penetrate our physical body, and become extra organs and the heaviness we carry everyday.

As part of the exhibition openning at Tillatec, Xuxu and Kiki developed the performance - Mobius Scores, interacting with the installation.
