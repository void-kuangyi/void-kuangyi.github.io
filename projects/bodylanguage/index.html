Body Language
Year: 2024
Commission
Interactive installation
touchdesigner, led screen, orbbec depth camera, acoustic drum trigger, leap motion hand tracker, abelton, midi

Together with Dutch singer Rhiannin and her band, we explored how to transform body language into an immersive visual and audio experience, and developed a LED installation responding visually and audibly to her movements and emotions on the stage.

The hand movement is tracked with a Leap Motion hand tracker and acts as a functional theremin, controlling electronic sound.
The body is tracked by an Orbbec depth camera and turned into real-time visuals, reflecting her emotions on the stage.
The drum is tracked by acoustic triggers, translating beats and emotions into live visuals
