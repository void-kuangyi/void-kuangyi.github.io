Kuangyi Xing is a designer, coder and artist based in Amsterdam. Their work examines how power dynamics between the individual and the system is reflected in body and desire, metaphors and symbols, history and heritage.

They use mediums like moving images, creative coding, electronics, performance, sculpture and latex, to create a magic circle and a space for us. They depict absurd, conflicting, grotesque visuals, alongside poetic aesthetics.
