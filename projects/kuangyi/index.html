Kuangyi Xing (they/them) is a designer, coder and media artist based in Amsterdam. Their work explores how the power dynamic between the individual and the system leaves traces in body, desire and history, through playfulness, satire, metaphors, and storytelling.

They use moving images, creative coding, electronics, performance, sculpture, textile, and latex, in a poetic and grotesque manner, to create a multi-sensorial magic circle.
