Weave
Year: 2023
Interactive installation
acrylic, threads, wood, 3d print, camera filter, film

Weave is an interactive data map of the invisible barriers immigrants face when navigating Dutch public systems. Built by its audience, it gradually reveals how structural barriers are often mistaken for personal failure.

This project grows out of my lived experience as an immigrant over 10 years. I developed the conflict map which shows how underlying factors, like cultural distance, close social network and homophile phenomenon contributes to the invisible barriers.
Metaphors run through the map, like cultural iceberg, climbing the mountains and seeing from others' eyes.
Through the red filter, the map reads differently, revealing every hidden message behind.
Dutch Design Week 2023
