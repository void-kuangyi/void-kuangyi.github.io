Do you see what I see
Year: 2024
Interactive installation
light, resin, arduino, switch, 3d print

Do you see what I see translates research data about different stakeholder perspectives on neighbourhood heritage to an interactive light installation.

Credits
Concept: Kuangyi Xing
Research: Spoormans et al. (2023)
Production: Kuangyi Xing
Photograph: Nas Hosen
Supported by: Nexum Data4Art
Audiences can interact, observe, and tangibly experience the variation of perspectives between and within stakeholder groups. Inserting the matching lens reveals a different focus value from a stakeholder, like an architect or an owner.
Each of the seven neighbourhood heritage values, identity, memory, collectivity, activity, appearance, comfort, spatial, infra, location and object, becomes a light-based element, with its research data mapped to light intensity.
The neighbourhood research method from Spoormans et al. (2023) conducted at Amsterdam Zuid-oost was applied to my own neighbourhood, Lombok, Utrecht.
