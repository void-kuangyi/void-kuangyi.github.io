Hairtwirling with me
Year: 2026
Interactive installation
esp32, osc control, rotary encoder, human hair, hair clip, resin, film, touchdesigner

If you know me in person, you literally can’t ignore that I twirl my hair constantly, a subconscious habit I had since childhood. Only after my diagnosis this year, I realised that that subtle movement is a stimming, self-soothe, sensory processing behaviour of autism.

I made a hair twirling device that brings this minor body gesture to attention, playing it, honouring it, rebelling the stigma associated with stimming and neurodivergence.

Tetem, Enschede
