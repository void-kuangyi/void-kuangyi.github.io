Gaze
Year: 2026
Video, 1'42"

Gaze is a performative media work on body resistance against daily gaze. It is based on a traumatic sexual harassment event of mine, depicting the itchy, paralysed, hysteria feeling when gazed, a subtle but harmful daily encounter.

The work is based on my own story of being sexually harassed and then unethically dismissed during my internship in Canberra, Australia in 2016. When I ideated about the work - Gaze, I had a flash back of the gaze from the perpetrator, managers and HRs sitting in the meeting room, judging the legitmacy of my story, and many gazes I received as a person of colour in public. Those gaze overlapped and merged, eventually transforming into the same look in their eyes.
