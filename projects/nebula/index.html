Nebula
Year: 2021
Interaction Design
GPS, compass, esp32, vibration motors, textile

Nebula is a haptic wayfinding belt for the visually impaired. By translating navigational data into haptic sensation, it supports unfamiliar urban exploration.

Credits
Concept: Kuangyi Xing & Daan Sonnemans
Design: Daan Sonnemans
Hardware: Kuangyi Xing & Daan Sonnemans
Software: Kuangyi Xing
Testing: Simon Dogger
Set a waypoint before you set off. Once turning on the wayfinding mode, The seven motors around the waist point the way back, using the embedded GPS and compass. The closer you get to the waypoint, the stronger the vibration.
