Wuchang
Year: 2026 - ongoing
Artistic Research
latex, 3d print, 3d scan, projection

Wuchang is a traditional ritualistic folk dance from my hometown, Gaochun, China. The project began as an inquiry into how the body and identity transform during ritual, which, by Johan Huizinga's definition in Homo Ludens, is also a form of play. From there, it opened onto the relationship between the ritual mask, digital identity, and neurodivergent masking.

Tetem, Enschede
