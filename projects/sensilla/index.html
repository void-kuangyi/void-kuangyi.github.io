Edaphic-Tox Sensilla
Year: 2026
Commission
Mask
textile, latex, soil, hair, thread, stapler

Edaphic-Tox Sensilla is a speculative sensing instrument from the year 2361, a megamachine era after the global climate apocalypse. Future human use it to sense soil toxicity in Amsterdam’s rediscovered relics from 2026.

The instrument is a symbiotic computer-organism made of microbial memory, apian antenna, fibre transmission, and concurrent swarm computing.

The instrument transformed the performer into a degenerated cyborg during Gunk Space's 47th annual symposium of post-archaeological artefacts between 29th Oct 29 and 05 November 2361.
